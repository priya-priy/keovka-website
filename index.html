OUR STORY

Rooted in Familiar Flavours.
Reimagined for Today.

Keovka brings together the comfort of familiar Indian flavours with a contemporary premium experience. From what goes inside to how it is presented, every detail is created to make ordinary moments feel a little more special.

We grew up in homes where sharing dry fruits was an unspoken gesture of warmth, blessings, and celebration. Yet modern snacking had become either overly commercial or uninspired. Keovka was born out of a desire to bring elegance and intention back to the table — crafting a singular, balanced food indulgence worthy of modern homes.
